## 1. Exact statements

* **Prop 1.4 (l.1752–1806), synchronization of geometric cyclotomes.** `X` proper, `char k = 0`,
  `1 → Δ_U → Π_U → G_k → 1`.
  * (i) (l.1764) for `x ∈ X(k) \ U(k)`, `U_x = X \ {x}`: "the inertia group `I_x` of `x` in
    `Δ_U` is naturally isomorphic to `Ẑ(1)`; the kernels of the natural surjections
    `Δ_U ↠ Δ_{U_x}`, `Π_U ↠ Π_{U_x}` are topologically normally generated by the inertia groups of
    points of `U_x \ U`".
  * (ii) (l.1771) `1 → I_x → Δ^{c-cn}_{U_x} → Δ_X → 1` (maximal cuspidally central quotient); the
    `E₂`-differential of `1 ∈ Hom(I_x, I_x)` gives `M_X := Hom(H²(Δ_X, Ẑ), Ẑ) ≅ I_x`.
* **Def 1.5 (l.1807–1836), Kummer-faithful.** `char k = 0`; `k` is Kummer-faithful (resp. torally
  Kummer-faithful) if for every finite `k_H ⊆ k̄` and every semi-abelian variety (resp. torus)
  `A/k_H`: (a) `⋂_{N≥1} N · A(k_H) = {0}`, equivalently (b) the Kummer map
  `A(k_H) → H¹(H, Hom(ℚ/ℤ, A(k̄)))` is injective.
  * Rem 1.5.1 (l.1837): torally KF ⇒ `χ_l` has open image.
  * Rem 1.5.2 (l.1856): one may restrict to `k_H = k` (Weil restriction).
  * Rem 1.5.3(i) (l.1868–1886): algebraic extensions of number fields with a nonarchimedean
    prime unramified over a number field and `μ(k†)` finite for all finite `k†/k` are torally KF.
  * Rem 1.5.4(i) (l.1910): sub-`p`-adic ⇒ Kummer-faithful; (ii) (l.1924): finitely generated
    extensions of (torally) KF fields are (torally) KF.
* **Prop 1.6 (l.1969–2039), Kummer classes of rational functions** (`k` Kummer-faithful).
  `κ_U : Γ(U, O_U^×) → H¹(Π_U, M_X)`; `J^d` = degree-`d` component of `Pic_{X/k}`.
  * (i) (l.1988) `κ_U` is injective.
  * (ii) (l.1993) `t_D : G_k → Π_{J^d}` from the sections `t_x`; for `d = 0`, `t_D` is the
    identity section (up to `Δ_X`-conjugacy) iff `D` is principal.
  * (iii) (l.2001) `U = X \ S`, `S ⊆ X(k)` finite: exact sequence
    `1 → (k^×)^∧ → H¹(Π_U, M_X) → ⊕_{x∈S} Ẑ` (restriction to the `I_x`, `Hom(I_x, M_X) ≅ Ẑ` by
    Prop 1.4(ii)); the image of `Γ(U, O^×)` in `H¹(Π_U, M_X)/(k^×)^∧` is the inverse image of the
    principal divisors supported in `S`.
  * Proof (l.2026–2039): (i) from Rem 1.5.4(ii) (`K_X` is torally KF); (iii) reduces (as in
    [Cusp] Prop 2.1(ii)) to `H⁰(G_k, Δ_X^ab) = 0`, which follows from Kummer-faithfulness for `J`.
* **Def 1.7 (l.2040–2050; Rem 1.7.1 l.2051).** NF-curves, NF-points, NF-rational functions,
  NF-constants.
* **Prop 1.8 (l.2061–2106).** `U` an NF-curve, `P_U ⊆ H¹(Π_U, M_X)` the inverse image of the
  cuspidal principal divisors.
  * (i) `η ∈ P_U` is the Kummer class of a nonconstant NF-rational function iff some positive
    multiple `η†` and NF-points `x₁, x₂ ∈ U(k_x)` have `η†|x₁ = 0`, `η†|x₂ ≠ 0`
    (`η|x := s_x^*(η) ∈ H¹(G_{k_x}, M_X)`).
  * (ii) if nonconstant NF-functions exist, `η ∈ P_U ∩ H¹(G_k, M_X)` is the Kummer class of an
    NF-constant iff `κ_U(f)|x = η|_{G_{k_x}}` for some nonconstant NF `f` and NF-point `x`.
  * Proof (l.2099–2106): nonconstant `f` on `X_NF` is surjective on `k̄_NF`-points.

  curve goes into **curves-jacobian** (`CurvesJacobian/Kummer/`), never the other way round;
